# ☕ Coffee Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes coffee sales data to uncover key business insights related to revenue performance, customer behavior, and market distribution. The analysis is conducted using Excel for data processing and dashboard creation, providing a clear and interactive view of sales performance across different countries and time periods.

## 🎯 Objectives
1. Analyze overall sales performance and key metrics
2. Identify revenue trends over time
3. Understand customer purchasing behavior
4. Evaluate market performance by country
5. Provide actionable business recommendations

## 📂 Dataset
The dataset contains transactional coffee sales data, including:
1. Order details and customer information
2. Product sales and quantities
3. Revenue data
4. Country-level sales distribution
5. Time-based sales records

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Excel for data analysis and dashboard creation

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights
The dashboard presents key business metrics through interactive visuals, including:
1. Monthly revenue trends to identify seasonality
2. Top 5 customers based on total spending
3. Revenue distribution by country
4. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for overall performance

## 📈 Key Insights
1. The United States dominates revenue, contributing approximately 79% of total sales, making it the primary market for the business.
2. Sales show clear seasonality, with peak revenue occurring in February, indicating strong demand during this period.
3. The top 5 customers contribute a relatively small portion of total revenue (~3.5%), suggesting a broad customer base rather than heavy reliance on a few high-value clients.
4. Ireland represents a growing market with nearly 15% of total revenue, indicating strong potential for expansion.
5. Customer spending levels are relatively consistent among top buyers, highlighting an opportunity to increase customer lifetime value through loyalty programs or targeted offers.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
1. Focus marketing and distribution efforts on the U.S. market to maximize revenue growth
2. Align promotions and inventory planning with seasonal demand patterns
3. Implement loyalty programs to improve customer retention and increase repeat purchases
4. Expand marketing efforts in Ireland to capitalize on its growth potential

## 📌 Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Excel can be used to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights through structured analysis and dashboard visualization. By identifying key trends in revenue, customer behavior, and market performance, the analysis provides a strong foundation for strategic decision-making and business growth.
